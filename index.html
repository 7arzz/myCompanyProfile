About Artheva

Artheva Artheva company is a DIY glass company that wants to continue to move forward, develop, and provide space for creativity in the process of making its products.

The word "Art" connotes beauty, creativity, and boundless expression. Just as art brings life to an atmosphere, we want this company to be a platform for beautiful ideas born from the heart and with meaning.

"Eva" comes from Latin and means life or life-giver. It represents positive energy, sustainable growth, and balance between humans and nature. Eva also symbolizes caring, that every work must be born from a sense of love that grows from within ourselves..

Star: Excellence, direction, quality standards, and premium identity.The letters A & V: Ambition + Vision, connected to each other, symbolize strength, creativity, and the brand's foundation.
